How to Pour Over
first time? start here
1
Put a paper filter in the Chemex. Pour hot water through it to rinse, then tip that water out. This rinses away any papery taste and warms the glass.
2
Put the Chemex on a kitchen scale and set it to grams. Add the ground coffee (the app tells you how many grams), give it a gentle shake so it sits flat, then set the scale back to zero.
3
Boil water and let it rest about 30 seconds. Tap Begin brewing on the app.
4
The Bloom: Slowly pour just enough water to wet all the coffee (the app shows the amount). It will puff up and bubble — that’s a good sign. Let it rest.
5
The Pours: When the app moves to Pour 1, 2, and 3, slowly add water in gentle circles, starting in the middle and spiraling outward. Watch the scale and pour until it reaches the gram number the app shows for each pour.
6
Drawdown: Stop pouring and let it all drain through. When the dripping stops, you’re done. Lift out the filter, swirl, and pour.
★
Go slow and steady — the gentle, unhurried pour is the whole secret. It doesn’t need to be perfect to taste wonderful.
