How to Pour Over
FIRST TIME? START HERE.
1
Put a paper filter in the Chemex. Pour hot water through it to rinse, then tip that water out. This washes away the papery taste and warms the glass.
2
Add the ground coffee (the app tells you how many grams). Give it a gentle shake so it sits flat.
3
Boil water and let it rest about 30 seconds. Tap Start brew on the app.
4
The Bloom: Slowly pour just enough water to wet all the coffee (the app shows the amount). It will puff up and bubble — that's good. Wait while it sits.
5
The Pours: When the app moves to Pour 1, 2, and 3, slowly add water in circles, starting in the middle and spiraling out. Keep the water level below the top. Pour up to the number the app shows each time.
6
Drawdown: Stop pouring and let all the water drain through. When it's done dripping, you're finished. Lift out the filter, swirl, and pour.
★
Go slow and steady — pouring gently is the whole trick. It doesn't have to be perfect to taste great.
